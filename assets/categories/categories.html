Automatic Door Operator

An automatic door operator is one of the most useful door hardware options. It can turn your manually-operated door into an automatic door. Automatic door operators are attached to the top of the door and, when triggered, they open the door so that the user doesn't have to open it themselves. The operator waits for a set period of time to walk through the door, and then it securely closes the door behind them.Here's what you need to know about the different types of automatic doors and the benefits you can get from installing one on your door.
